# Made with ARTdeFACTS

ARTdeFACTS is a free, open-source Obsidian plugin for the desktop that turns your Markdown notes into designed pages, slide decks and Boards you fill in; your notes stay plain Markdown in your vault, and nothing leaves it unless you export or publish it.

## What it does

- **Show**: one note becomes a page, in one of dozens of styles, light and dark.
- **Present**: the same note as slides, fullscreen, with a clock and a timer.
- **Track**: tables and checklists become fields you click: choices, sliders, dates, totals, time tracking with a timeline.
- **Organize**: tabs, columns of cards, lists side by side.
- **Publish**: on your own GitHub Pages or Codeberg Pages site, like this one. A page can be locked with a phrase.

Everything stays plain Markdown in your vault.

## Get it

1. Install [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), free for personal use.
2. In Obsidian, install [BRAT](https://github.com/TfTHacker/obsidian42-brat) from the community plugins.
3. In BRAT, choose **Add beta plugin** and enter `cg-commits/ARTdeFACTS`.

Everything about it, from the first steps to every style: [the ARTdeFACTS repository](https://github.com/cg-commits/ARTdeFACTS).

If it helps you, [support it on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/christiangajda).
